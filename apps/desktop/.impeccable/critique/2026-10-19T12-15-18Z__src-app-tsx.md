## Overall Impression

Convincing DJ instrument over an honest-data skeleton that hasn't arrived: PRNG waveform peaks, duration-percent phrases, hardcoded -8.4 LUFS, oscillator click-track preview, all rendered with the visual authority of real analysis. Biggest opportunity: make the app honest.

## What's Working

1. Domain-authentic control design in PreviewPlayer (CDJ-correct CUE, color pads, linked overview map, snap badge).
2. Reversibility patterns where implemented: filter draft/Apply with inline validation, enrichment preview → diff → selective apply → Undo run, distinct "no tracks" vs "no matches" empty states.
3. Coherent token-driven visual system (data-theme palette, consistent grammar, teal=audio/orange=cue, central :focus-visible and prefers-reduced-motion).

## Persona Red Flags

- Alex (power user): one-at-a-time cue setting, no multi-select/templates, advertised accelerators missing (zoom keys, cmd-K), wheel zoom lies. Abandon risk high.
- Sam (a11y): role="slider" waveforms keyboard-inoperable (WCAG 2.1.1), Space hijacks focused pads, muted text 3.3-4.2:1 fails AA (detector-confirmed), color-only phrase bands, modals lack role="dialog"/focus trap/return, no aria-live status.
